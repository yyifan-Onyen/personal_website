I am a Ph.D. Student at the [University of Georgia](https://www.uga.edu/), where I am fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Tianming Liu](https://experts.uga.edu/tianming.liu/about). My research focuses on **LLM-based agents**, **multi-agent systems**, and **agentic reinforcement learning** — building agents that can reason, collaborate, and continually improve through interaction.

Research Interests

My research interests lie in:

- **LLM-based Agents**
- **Multi-agent Systems**
- **Agentic Reinforcement Learning (Agentic RL)**
