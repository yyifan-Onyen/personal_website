About

I am a Ph.D. Student at the University of Georgia, where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Tianming Liu. My work centers on large language model agents — building systems that can reason, collaborate, and improve through interaction.

Research Interests

My research interests lie in:

  • LLM-based Agents
  • Multi-agent Systems
  • Agentic Reinforcement Learning (Agentic RL)
